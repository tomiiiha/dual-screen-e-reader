# 001 — Initial Design Concept

Date: 2026-10-15
Status: Preliminary

## Concept
A folding dual screen e-reader with two e-paper displays, physical controls, and adjustable frontlighting.

## Motivation
The design aims to combine the portability and convenience of commercial e-readers with a more traditional two page reading experience.

The project will emphasize original enclosure and electronics design while allowing the use of existing open source software.

## Initial Decisions
- Two displays rather than a single display.
- Physical controls instead of a touchscreen.
- Adjustable frontlighting (important).
- Transparent or translucent enclosure preferred.
- Folding design for display protection.

## Unresolved Decisions
- Display dimensions and technology.
- System processor and memory.
- Battery and charging system.
- Hinge construction.
- Software platform.

## Next Steps
Evaluate candidate displays and develop a preliminary system architecture.
