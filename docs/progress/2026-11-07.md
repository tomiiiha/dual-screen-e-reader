# Progress Update — November 7, 2026

CrossPoint is running on the ESP32-S3 with the 5.76-inch Good Display GDEH0576T81 E Ink panel. The display, microSD breakout, and ANO navigation controller are working together on the breadboard.

## Working now

- CrossPoint boots into its main menu.
- The SD card mounts, and the library shows the test files.
- Plain text opens and is readable with text anti-aliasing turned off.
- All five ANO navigation buttons work.
- Turning the ANO rotary wheel in either direction acts as Back.

## SD card troubleshooting

The reader started showing `SD card error` after a reset. Reformatting the card and reflashing CrossPoint didn't solve it.

I tested the SD card separately with a small PlatformIO program. The test could mount the card and list the `books` folder. I then reconnected the E Ink display and the ANO controller one at a time; the SD test kept passing. After uploading CrossPoint again, the menu and library returned.

The original failure hasn't been explained yet. The standalone test confirms that the hardware can work, but I still need to check whether startup stays reliable over repeated resets.

## Text rendering

Text looked fine on the first draw, then became almost inverted after a second refresh. Turning off **Text Anti-Aliasing** in CrossPoint stopped that behavior.

My working theory is that the custom display driver doesn't handle CrossPoint's grayscale rendering pass correctly. For now, anti-aliasing needs to stay off. This is a workaround, not a driver fix.

## Controls

The ANO has five navigation switches: center for Select, plus Up, Down, Left, and Right. I connected its encoder outputs to GPIO 18 and 21 and added a temporary Back action in `src/main.cpp`. Either direction of rotation triggers Back.

This works for testing, although the final controls may change once the enclosure is designed.
